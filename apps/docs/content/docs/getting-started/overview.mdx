It takes about an evening to write a good page. You can stop at any point and come back; nothing is visible to anyone until you publish.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up with your own name and email, even if the page is for someone else. You can sign in later with a password, an emailed link or a passkey.

Please confirm your email address when the link arrives. A page can only be confirmed or published from a confirmed address.

## 2. Say who the page is for

Rishta asks one question first: **Who is this page for?**

- **Me**: you are the candidate. You own the household and hold the seal.
- **My son, my daughter, my brother, my sister, or someone else in my family**: you are starting a page for them. Add their first name and, when you're ready, their email address.

<Callout title="A page a relative starts stays private">
	If you start a page for someone else, they receive an invitation to read it and confirm it.
	Until they do, the page appears in nobody's folio, and the folio stays closed: "The folio opens
	when Priya confirms her page." They can also decline, kindly, and the draft is deleted.
</Callout>

## 3. Set your non-negotiables

Next comes **The first page**, five short steps, in this order:

1. **Timeline**: when you hope to marry, and whether you'd like to meet a man or a woman.
2. **Where**: whether you're open to relocating, residency, and the places you'd consider.
3. **Age, community and education**.
4. **Faith and diet**, each marked as a dealbreaker or a nice-to-have.
5. **What matters most**: twelve points to spread across looks, personality and financial stability. You can't spend more than twelve, which is the point.

You can change these later on your **Looking for** page. Changes affect tomorrow's folio, not today's.

## 4. Write your page

**My Biodata** is your page, edited in place: what you see is exactly what others will see, what prints and what exports. The sections follow the traditional order: an optional invocation, your name, Personal, Education & work, Family, Lifestyle, About, Looking for and the Sealed section.

- Click or tap any value to edit it. It saves as you go.
- Each field can be **Shown**, **Sealed** (opens only after you both say yes) or **Matching only** (used to choose your folio, never shown). Faith and community ask for your consent explicitly.
- Add up to five photos. Strangers only ever see a veiled copy made on our servers.
- The margin tells you, in words, what's missing ("Family: 2 of 5").

## 5. Publish

When the required fields are complete, your page is claimed and your Looking for page is done, the **Publish** bar appears. Publish, and your first folio arrives that evening, at 7:00 pm in your time zone.

You can **pause** your page at any time. It leaves every folio at once, and your letters stay open.
