title: How Rishta works
description: A private, respectful way to be introduced for marriage, built around the biodata page your family already writes.
---

Rishta is built around one object: the **biodata page**, the one-page marriage profile that families already write and forward. Everything you do here is reading a page, writing a note to someone whose page moved you, or leaving a pencil note in a page's margin.

## The short version

1. **You write your page**, or a relative starts it for you and you confirm it. Nothing about you is visible to anyone until the page is published.
2. **Each evening a small folio arrives**: five to seven complete pages, chosen against your non-negotiables. You read them one at a time.
3. **To show interest, you write a note** of at least forty characters and press your seal onto it.
4. **When the other person says yes, both seals break together.** Photos, full names, workplaces and contact open to each other, and three evenings are proposed for a first call, in both time zones.
5. **Family helps in the margin**, through a private link in their own language, or with a seat in your household. Only you can write a note, say yes or close your search.

## Words you'll see

| Word               | What it means                                                                                   |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Page**           | One person's biodata, laid out as the document families know.                                   |
| **Folio**          | Today's five to seven pages, released each evening.                                             |
| **Kept pages**     | Pages you kept to read again or show your family.                                               |
| **Note**           | 40 to 400 characters, written to one person and pressed with your seal.                         |
| **Letter**         | A note and everything that follows it: the answer, the Introduction, the correspondence.        |
| **Seal**           | The round monogram on a note and on a page's sealed section. It is pending, pressed or broken.  |
| **Sealed section** | The lower part of every page: extra photos, full name, workplace, contact, exact date of birth. |
| **Introduction**   | What a yes creates: both seals broken, both sealed sections open, three evenings proposed.      |
| **Household**      | You and the family helping you.                                                                 |
| **Pencil note**    | A family member's reaction in a page's margin: Proceed, Let's talk or Not for us.               |
| **Family link**    | A private, watermarked, expiring link to one page, for someone without an account.              |
| **Why this page**  | Two to six plain reasons a page is in your folio, gaps included. Never a score.                 |
