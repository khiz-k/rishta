---
title: Reading your folio
description: Five to seven complete pages each evening, read one at a time. Keep, Pass or Write a note.
---

Your **folio** is today's small stack of pages: five on Free, seven on Premium. It arrives each evening at your folio hour (7:00 pm in your time zone unless you change it in Household settings) and stays until the next one arrives.

## Reading a page

Pages are read one at a time, top to bottom, in the traditional order. The position shows at the stack edge, for example **3 of 7**.

- **Turn a page** with a horizontal swipe on a phone, the **←** and **→** keys on a computer, or the chevrons in the margin.
- **Turning a page never answers it.** Nothing is decided by a swipe.

## Why this page

Every page comes with two to six plain reasons it is in your folio, and the honest gaps:

- "You both hope to marry within a year."
- "He's open to relocating; you're in Toronto."
- "Diet differs: you're vegetarian, he isn't."
- "Community: not stated on his page."

There are no percentages and no scores. The reasons are worked out the same way for everyone, from both people's non-negotiables. Nobody appears in your folio whose dealbreakers exclude you, and you never appear in theirs.

## Answering a page

Each page has three answers, in the margin bar:

| Answer           | What happens                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Keep**         | The page is saved to **Kept pages**, to read again or show your family.                                                                            |
| **Pass**         | The page leaves your folio quietly. You can add a private reason. There is a six-second Undo, and passed pages are listed at the end of the folio. |
| **Write a note** | Opens the seal composer. See [Writing and sealing a note](/guides/sealing-a-note).                                                                 |

A page you didn't answer may come back once, after a few days, marked "You've seen this page before".

## The end of the folio

When you've read every page: "That's today's folio. The next arrives tomorrow evening." The end slip also lists what you kept and wrote, and the pages you passed, in case you'd like to undo one.

## Kept pages

**Kept pages** use the same reader, newest first. Pencil notes from your family appear in the margin beside each page.
