---
title: Letters and Introductions
description: Answering a note, what a yes creates, the first call across two time zones, and closing kindly.
---

**Letters** hold everything that follows a note. There are four groups:

- **Waiting for your answer**: notes you've received, oldest first. Priority notes are pinned for two days and labelled.
- **Introductions**: the people you've both said yes to.
- **Your sealed notes**: notes you've sent that are waiting.
- **Closed**: letters that were declined, taken back or closed.

Rows never show photos, so a glance over your shoulder reveals nothing. Your letters are private to you: family members see "Priya's letters are private to her".

## Answering a note

Open a letter to read the note clipped to its page. You have three answers:

- **Say yes.** You'll be asked to confirm: "Say yes to Arjun? Both seals break, and your sealed section opens to him."
- **Decline kindly.** A gentle note is sent, which you can edit: "Thank you for writing. I don't think we're the right match, and I wish you well in your search." Nobody ever sees a red X.
- **Let it close quietly.** The sender sees only "This letter has closed."

Declines are final, and a letter nobody answers closes on its own after thirty days, so nobody is left guessing.

## Both seals break together

A yes creates an **Introduction** at once. Both seals break, and both sealed sections open to each other: clear photos, full names, workplaces, exact dates of birth and, if shared, contact details. The other person sees the seals break the next time they open the letter.

## The first call

The Introduction arrives with **three evenings that suit you both**: the next three dates when both of your local times fall between 6 and 10 pm.

- Tick the times that work for you. The first time you both tick is booked.
- Or **propose three others** of your own.
- When a call is booked, you both receive a calendar invite showing both local times: "Booked: Thursday 2 October, 8:00 pm Edison · 7:00 pm Dallas."

After the call, each of you can choose to share a family contact. Both are shown only once both have shared.

## Correspondence

Inside an Introduction you can write to each other, up to 2,000 characters a message. There are no typing indicators and no double ticks; a single quiet "Read" appears under your last message. If a week passes without a word, one pencil line appears: "It's been a week. Propose a call, or close kindly."

## Closing kindly

When you'd rather not continue, choose **Close kindly** and edit the closing note if you like. The other person sees "Arjun has decided not to continue. He wished you well." The thread stays readable for both of you, and messaging stops.
