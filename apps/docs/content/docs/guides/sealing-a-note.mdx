---
title: Writing and sealing a note
description: A real sentence, at least forty characters, pressed with your seal. How it works, and what the limits are.
---

On Rishta, interest costs a sentence. When a page moves you, you write a **note** to that person and press your **seal** onto it. There are no likes and no one-tap requests.

## Writing

- A note is **40 to 400 characters**. The salutation ("Dear Priya,") and your signature are added for you.
- The counter tells you where you are: "14 more characters and you can seal it", then "Ready to seal."
- Mention something from their page. The best notes show you read it.

If AI is available, you can ask for a **suggested first line** built from what your pages have in common. It is only a start: "Make the line your own before sealing." A suggestion can't be sent word for word.

<Callout title="Contact details stay sealed" type="warn">
	Phone numbers, email addresses, WhatsApp or Telegram handles and links can't be sent in a note.
	Contact opens only when you both say yes.
</Callout>

## Sealing

The seal unlocks at forty characters. Then press it in any of three ways:

- **Press and hold** the seal for a moment, while its ink spreads out to the gold rim.
- On a keyboard, **focus the seal and hold Enter or Space**.
- **Tap once, then confirm.**

"Sealed and sent. He'll read it with your page." Only the candidate, the person whose marriage it is, can seal a note. Family members never can.

## Limits and credits

|                          | Free | Premium |
| ------------------------ | ---- | ------- |
| Notes each day           | 3    | 10      |
| Credits added each month | none | 2       |

The day runs from midnight in your household's time zone. Past the limit, **one credit** buys one extra note. A new household starts with ten free credits, and more can be bought in **Credits & plan** ($5 for five).

## Priority notes

For one credit, a note can be sent as a **priority note**. It is labelled "Priority note" for the reader and sits at the top of her letters for two days. It is always disclosed, it is flat (there are no bids or amounts), and only one can be active between the same two people.

## Taking a note back

While a note is still waiting for an answer, you can take it back from **Your sealed notes** in Letters. The other person sees "Arjun took back his note."
