Family takes part in the margin. They read, keep pages and react; only the candidate writes notes, says yes or closes the search.

## Show my family: family links

From any page in your folio, your kept pages or a letter you've received, choose **Show my family** to make a private link to that one page.

1. Say who it's for ("Ammi", "Papa", "Nani").
2. Choose their language: English, Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil or Telugu.
3. Choose how long it stays open: one, three or seven days.
4. Share it on WhatsApp or any other way.

The link opens **without an account**, at a larger size, in the language you chose, clearly marked as a translation with a way to see the original. It carries a watermark naming who it was shared with and asks them not to forward it.

Under the page are three square buttons: **Proceed**, **Let's talk** and **Not for us**. Their answer appears beside the page as a pencil note, signed "Ammi (via link)". They can change it until the link closes, and it never changes a letter's state.

<Callout title="If a page shouldn't be shared">
	Anyone can choose that their page is never shown on family links. Their page then says "This
	family prefers their page isn't shared by link".
</Callout>

## Your household

A household is the candidate plus the family helping them. Invite people from **Household** in the avatar menu, and give each a relation label ("Ammi", "Bhaiya").

|                                   | Candidate | Guardian   | Family     |
| --------------------------------- | --------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| Read the folio and kept pages     | yes       | yes        | if allowed |
| Keep pages and write pencil notes | yes       | yes        | yes        |
| Edit the page                     | yes       | if allowed | no         |
| Invite family                     | yes       | yes        | no         |
| Write, seal or answer a note      | **yes**   | no         | no         |
| Read letters and messages         | **yes**   | no         | no         |
| Close the search                  | **yes**   | no         | no         |

Free households have room for the candidate and two family members; Premium for the candidate and six.

## Starting a page for someone else

If you started the page, you can keep drafting while you wait for the candidate to confirm it. When they do, they become the owner of the household, and you become its guardian. If they decline, the draft is deleted and you receive a kind email; no reason is asked for.
