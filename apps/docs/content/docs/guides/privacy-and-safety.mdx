Rishta is built so that nothing about you is exposed before you say yes.

## What stays private

- **Photos are veiled** until you both say yes. The veil is a small blurred copy made on our servers, so a stranger never receives the clear picture.
- **The sealed section** (full name, workplace, phone, exact date of birth, extra photos) opens only inside an Introduction.
- **Reading privately** stops the people whose pages you read from being told. It's free.
- **Blocks work both ways.** The other person's page, letters, messages, readers entries and family links disappear for you, and yours for them.
- **Email subjects never name anyone**: "A letter is waiting for you", "Your letter has an answer".

## Readers

**Readers**, from My Biodata, lists who read your page and when: "Harpreet Singh read your page · Tuesday". There are no totals and no photos, and blocked people never appear.

## Reporting

"Tell us what happened. We read every report, and we won't tell them you reported."

You can report from any page, letter, message or family link. Choose what went wrong (asking for money, harassment, a fake page, someone under 18, pressure to marry, hateful words, or something else), add details if you like, and choose whether to block them too. A person on our team reviews every report and may pause a page while they look into it.

When a note looks like a common scam, for example one that asks you to move to WhatsApp before you've spoken, you'll see a quiet safety line beside it: "Take care: this note asks you to move to WhatsApp before you've spoken."

<Callout title="What we don't do" type="warn">
	Rishta does not conduct criminal background checks on its members. We verify email addresses.
	The same service and prices apply to everyone, regardless of gender or nationality.
</Callout>

## Close my search

From the avatar menu, choose **Close my search**, then one of three reasons:

- **We're engaged.** "Congratulations. We'll close your page kindly and let your open conversations know." Your page leaves every folio, waiting letters are declined with a kind note, and open Introductions are closed with thanks.
- **Taking a break.** Your page is paused and leaves every folio; your letters and Introductions stay open. Resume whenever you like.
- **Something else.** Your page is closed, kindly, in the same way.

## Your data

You can delete your account from your account settings at any time. The privacy policy, linked at the foot of every page on the Rishta website, explains what we keep, for how long, and why.
