_Draft for review. This policy describes how Rishta is built to handle your information. It is being reviewed by counsel before launch, and the final version may change the wording, but not the principles._

**Last updated: 27 September 2026**

Rishta helps people who want to marry, and the families who help them, read biodata pages and be introduced. A biodata holds some of the most personal information a person can share: faith, community, family, and what you hope for in a marriage. We collect as little as the service needs, we keep the most sensitive parts sealed until both people say yes, and we never sell any of it.

## Who we are

Rishta ("we", "us") operates the Rishta website and app. For the purposes of data protection law we are the controller of the personal information described here. You can write to us from the [contact page](/contact).

## What we collect

**Your account.** Your name, email address, password (stored only as a hash) or sign-in method, and your language and time zone.

**Your page.** Everything you, or a relative you have allowed, write on your biodata page: name, date of birth, height, marital history, languages, education and work, family details, lifestyle, an "About" section, photographs, and what you are looking for. Some of it is **special-category information** in data protection law, such as your faith, community or caste if you choose to state it. Each of these fields is optional and carries its own choice: _shown on my page_ or _used only for matching_.

**The sealed section.** Your full name, workplace, phone number, exact date and time of birth, and any extra photographs. These are stored separately and are shown only inside an Introduction, after both of you have said yes.

**Your household.** The family members you invite, the role each one has, the relation labels you give them ("Ammi"), and their pencil notes in the margin of pages.

**Letters.** The notes you write and receive, answers, the times you propose for a call, and the correspondence inside an Introduction.

**Family links.** When you share a page with someone who has no account, we record who it was shared with (the label you gave), when it expires, and the reaction they leave. We do not ask them for anything else.

**Reading.** Which pages you have read, so we do not show them to you again, and, unless you read privately, a line on the other person's Readers list. You can turn on private reading at any time; it is free.

**Payments.** If you buy Premium or credits, our payment processor (Stripe) handles your card. We receive only the plan, the amount, the status of the subscription and a customer reference.

**Safety.** Reports you file, and the notes our safety checks flag (for example, a request for money). Reports are read by people.

**Technical information.** Standard server logs (IP address, device and browser type, time of request) kept for security, and, only if you allow it, privacy-friendly analytics on the public website.

## How we use it

- To build your daily folio: pages are chosen deterministically against both people's non-negotiables. We do not infer your faith, community, complexion or family status, and there is no complexion field.
- To deliver notes, answers and Introductions, and to propose call times across time zones.
- To show a page to your family through a link, translated into their language when you ask, and always labelled as a translation.
- To keep members safe: screening notes for scams and pressure, handling reports, and enforcing blocks in both directions.
- To bill you, if you choose a paid plan.
- To send you email about your account and your letters. Email subjects never name anyone.

**Our legal bases** are your consent (for special-category fields, which you can withdraw by removing them), the contract between us (to provide the service), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe.

## AI

Where AI helps (a first draft of your page, a suggested first line for a note, translation for your family, a safety flag on a note you received), it produces a visible draft that you can edit or ignore. It never decides, never sends and never rates anyone's looks. When it runs, the relevant text is processed by our AI provider under a contract that forbids using it to train their models.

## Who can see what

- **Strangers** see your page only in their folio or through a link, with photographs replaced by a veil made on our servers. They never receive the clear image. Your sealed section is closed.
- **The person you are introduced to** sees your sealed section and clear photographs, after you have both said yes.
- **Your household** sees the pages in your folio and the pages you keep. Family members never read your letters or messages.
- **Someone holding a family link** sees one page, watermarked with their name, until the link expires.
- **Blocked people** see nothing of you, and you see nothing of them.
- **A page a relative drafts** is visible to no one outside the household until the adult it describes confirms it.

## Who we share it with

We use processors to run the service: hosting and databases, file storage for photographs, email delivery, payments (Stripe), and our AI provider. Each is bound by contract to use your information only to provide their service to us. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising.

We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect someone from serious harm.

## How long we keep it

We keep your page while your search is open. When you close your search, your page leaves every folio at once. If you delete your account, we delete your page, photographs and letters within 30 days, except where we must keep a record of a report or a payment for legal reasons.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act), you can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, export it, delete it, or restrict or object to how we use it. You can withdraw consent for any special-category field by removing it from your page. Most of this you can do yourself in Settings; for the rest, write to us from the contact page. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

Rishta is only for adults. You must be 18 or older to confirm or publish a page, and we remove pages about anyone younger.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you by email before the change takes effect.
