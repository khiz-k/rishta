## What Rishta is

Rishta is a matchmaking service for adults who want to marry, and for the family members who help them. It shows you a small folio of biodata pages each evening, lets you write a note to someone whose page you would like to answer, and introduces you when you both say yes.

## Who can use it

- You must be at least 18 years old, and legally able to marry where you live.
- You must be looking for marriage, for yourself or for an adult in your family, and you must tell the truth on the page.
- A page a relative writes for you is not published until you confirm it. Only the adult a page describes can confirm it, write notes, say yes or close the search.
- You may not use Rishta if we have removed you before, or if you are prohibited by law from doing so.

## Your household

A household is the candidate plus the family helping them. The candidate owns the household. Guardians can help write the page and invite family; family members can read and leave pencil notes. Nobody in a household can write, send or accept a note for the candidate, or read the candidate's letters.

## Your page and your words

You own what you write and the photographs you upload. You give us a licence to store, display and, when you ask, translate them, only to run Rishta for you. You confirm you have the right to share every photograph, and that nobody in them objects.

Write about yourself honestly. Do not include another person's contact details, and do not state things about others that you are not sure are true.

## How to behave

Rishta only works if people treat each other with respect. You agree not to:

- ask anyone for money, gifts, or financial or immigration help;

- write anything hateful about anyone's faith, caste, community, colour, gender or nationality;
- impersonate anyone, or create a page about someone without their knowledge;
- copy, forward or publish another member's page, photographs or messages outside Rishta, including a family link shared with you;

We may remove content, pause a page or close an account that breaks these rules, and we will tell you why unless doing so would put someone at risk.

## Safety

**Rishta does not conduct criminal background checks on its members.** We verify email addresses, screen notes for common signs of scams, and read every report. Please read our [safety page](/safety) before you meet anyone, and never send money to someone you have met through Rishta.

The same service and the same prices apply to everyone, regardless of gender or nationality.

## Plans, credits and payment

- **Free**: a folio of five pages each evening and three notes a day, with every safety feature.
- **Premium**: billed to the household, monthly or yearly, with the price shown before you buy. Premium includes a free trial the first time; if you do not cancel before it ends, the subscription begins and renews automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.
- **Credits**: bought in packs. One credit is an extra note on a busy day or a priority note, which is always labelled for the reader. Credits do not expire while your account is open and are not refundable once used.

You can cancel Premium online at any time from Credits & plan, and it will stay active until the end of the period you have paid for. Where the law gives you a right to a refund, we honour it.
